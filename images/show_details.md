Show 1: Macbeth: A King's Descent
Genre: Shakespearean Tragedy & Political Thriller
Description: Step into the brutal world of ancient Scotland with this visceral production of Shakespeare’s ultimate cautionary tale. We trace the warrior Macbeth's rapid descent from noble hero to bloodthirsty tyrant, driven by a manipulative, ambitious wife and a chilling, dark prophecy that seals his doom. Experience the terrifying cost of absolute power and the relentless madness that follows political murder in this gripping, timeless psychological thriller.

Show 2: The Clockwork Menagerie
Genre: Steampunk Fantasy Musical
Description: Enter a whimsical, alternate-Victorian city powered entirely by brilliant clockwork ingenuity and artistic artifice. Our story follows a reclusive genius inventor and his extraordinary collection of sentient mechanical creatures—from tiny automata birds to a reasoning clockwork sphinx—all existing in a delicate, automated ecosystem. When a young, aspiring engineer accidentally unlocks the central, sentient clockwork heart, she must partner with the menagerie to save their mechanical paradise from a ruthless industrial threat.

Show 3: Echoes of Silence
Genre: Contemporary Ballet & Physical Theatre
Description: In a non-verbal narrative of incredible physical control and emotional depth, this piece explores the visceral struggle for connection in an age defined by digital noise and profound, underlying isolation. Our ensemble of world-class dancers creates a dynamic, moving dialogue about human vulnerability, using silence itself as a powerful, potent force for communication. From moments of ecstatic collective movement to quiet, solitary despair, this production offers a powerful, contemporary meditation on what it truly means to be human together.
